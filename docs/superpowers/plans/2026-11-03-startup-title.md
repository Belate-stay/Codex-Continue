# Shared startup title implementation plan

**Goal:** Connect the splash and connection screens with one continuous software-name transition, followed by a restrained logo and connection-content entrance. Preserve Codex-Continue branding and Beta 0.9.1.

**Design:** Animate the actual title-bar name from the measured splash-title bounds using scale and translation, then settle it at its natural header size. Raise the transparent title-bar layer above the opaque connection page so the word remains visible along its entire path. Hide the central copy in the same frame; fade the start button out. Keep the connection canvas opaque throughout. Per the user's motion refinement, the title starts slowly and accelerates through a 640 ms first segment, covering 94.4 percent of the distance, then lands through an 80 ms deceleration with approximately matched speed. Reveal the vector logo and connection heading, ring, footer and skip control through overlapping eased entrances from 600 ms; the sequence finishes at 1030 ms. No bitmap scaling cache on the moving title, new asset, dependency or preference.

**Architecture:** A focused `MainWindow.StartupMotion.cs` partial owns one controllable storyboard and its cleanup. The title remains attached to its real header anchor, which makes the endpoint stable during resize/maximize/DPI changes. Wrap the connection heading/shadow to animate them together without applying an effect to live text. Connection probing and its existing five-second gate run independently of motion. Retry keeps the header in place. Skip, tray settings, release details, hide and close settle/cancel the storyboard without stale callbacks; a skipped connection preserves its current content pose while exiting. Reduced motion settles immediately.
